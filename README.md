### Featured project: EpiChron

An **epigenetic clock** that predicts biological age from DNA methylation.
**[Live site](https://epichron.vercel.app)** · [Code and write-up](https://github.com/akhdjoudi/epichron)

- **3.0 years MAE on 729 people from a cohort never seen at training** (a different lab), with 249 CpG sites out of ~450,000
- ElasticNet (3.0) beat a neural net (3.6) and LightGBM (4.0): with ~500 training samples, the simplest model won
- SHAP recovers known aging genes (ELOVL2, FHL2, KLF14) in two differently trained models
- The model runs in the browser, with no server; the site never accepts visitor DNA
- Limitations are documented in the README
